What is the ICT Silver Bullet?

The Silver Bullet is an intraday precision entry model by ICT (Inner Circle Trader). It uses three specific 1-hour windows where institutional order flow is most concentrated, then hunts a Fair Value Gap (FVG) in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias as the entry trigger. The model targets liquidity drawn from prior sessions and key daily levels, making it a clean example of the AMD (Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution) framework.

The Three Windows (New York Time)

🕒 London Open: 03:00–04:00 AM ET
🕙 NY AM: 10:00–11:00 AM ET
🕑 NY PM: 14:00–15:00 PM ET

Outside these windows → no trades. Each window resets independently.

Step-by-Step Entry Rules

  1. Determine 4H Bias — look at the last completed 4H candle before the window opens. If bullish → look for longs. If bearish → look for shorts.
  2. Wait for the window — only act within the 1-hour Silver Bullet window.
  3. Spot the first FVG — a 3-candle imbalance (gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low for bullish; candle 1 low and candle 3 high for bearish). This is your entry zone.
  4. Wait for retrace INTO the FVG — price must pull back into the gap before entry.
  5. Enter at close of retrace candle — the candle that trades back into the FVG.
  6. Stop Loss — below/above the left leg of the FVG plus a small buffer (5–15 pts).
  7. Take Profit — 2:1 RR (hard target from entry).
  8. Minimum TP — skip if TP distance < 10 pts (low-quality setups).

AMD Framework

The Silver Bullet lives inside the Manipulation phase of AMD:

  • Accumulation — Asia/pre-session range forms. Smart money builds positions quietly. Liquidity sits above highs and below lows.
  • Manipulation — The Silver Bullet windows capture the stop hunt move: price raids the prior session high/low to grab liquidity before reversing. The FVG forms on this impulse move.
  • Distribution — Price expands in the true direction toward the daily target (PDH/PDL, session highs/lows). This is where the TP is hit.

Backtest Parameters

  • Instruments: S&P 500, NASDAQ, DAX (M5)
  • Risk per trade: 1% of balance (compounding)
  • RR target: 2:1 (hard)
  • Starting balance: $10,000
  • Bias filter: 4H momentum (close vs close 2 bars ago)
  • FVG retrace window: up to 24 M5 candles after window close
  • Minimum TP distance: 10 pts
  • SL buffer: 5 pts (S&P), 15 pts (NASDAQ/DAX)

Key Stats

  • Average Win RR: exactly 2.0 (fixed TP)
  • Average Loss RR: ~−0.97 (close to 1R)
  • Breakeven WR needed: 33.3% (with 2:1 RR)
  • Actual WR: 39–43% → statistically positive edge
  • Best window: NY AM (10–11 AM ET) across all instruments
